Lamborghini is an iconic Italian manufacturer of high-performance luxury sports cars, founded by Ferruccio Lamborghini in 1963 to compete with Ferrari, known for aggressive designs, powerful engines, and innovative features like scissor doors (Lambo doors) on models like the Countach; now owned by Audi AG and part of the Volkswagen Group, the brand offers hybrid supercars like the Revuelto and the Urus Super SUV, embracing electrification under its "Direzione Cor Tauri" strategy.
